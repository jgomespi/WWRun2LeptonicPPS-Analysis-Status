CURRENT FOCUS

What is happening now?

The 2018 signal-region kinematic reconstruction is complete and audited. Before freezing the first blinded templates, a systematic-input consistency audit identified a correction needed in a derived uncertainty treatment while leaving the central nominal reconstruction unchanged. The correction has been validated and is now being propagated through a fresh 2018 derived production. The next gate is closure of that regenerated sample, followed by blinded templates and expected-only inference.
