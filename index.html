CURRENT FOCUS

What is happening now?

The 2018 publication model now includes the accepted detector, reconstruction, migration, forward-proton and statistical uncertainty treatments required by the current analysis scope. Per-channel and combined workspaces are reproducible, expected-only inference is operational, and the background-only Asimov fit closes successfully while the observed signal region remains blinded.

The remaining 2018 work is concentrated in the final statistical-validation layer: profile-likelihood scans, nuisance diagnostics, pre/post-fit validation, goodness-of-fit, channel consistency, sparse-bin stability and the final provenance freeze.
