CURRENT FOCUS

What is happening now?

The 2018 analysis is technically publication-complete while remaining blinded. Nominal reconstruction, detector and forward-proton systematics, statistical-model validation, channel-consistency checks, goodness-of-fit studies and deterministic provenance closure are complete.

The active work is now the propagation of that frozen architecture to the remaining Run 2 periods. The 2017 workflow has completed its main processing and calibration stages and is closing generator-level normalization bookkeeping before the final 2017 normalization contract and nominal derived production are frozen. The same contracts will then be propagated to 2016.
